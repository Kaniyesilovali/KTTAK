Our criteria

Why a document makes it onto this shelf

You can use the same criteria when deciding whether to trust a source. More in our misinformation guide.

  1. A known publisher

    It states clearly who prepared the document and on whose behalf it is published.

  2. The current edition

    If a newer edition exists, we link to that one, not the old one.

  3. Free to access

    It can be read without registration, payment or membership.

  4. Links to the source

    The link goes to the publisher's own page, not to a copy or mirror site.

Links checked on 8 November 2026. If you find a broken link or a new edition, or want to suggest a document for the shelf, write to us.
