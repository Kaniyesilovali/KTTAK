Nobody goes into the field untrained

Our volunteering does not begin on the day of a disaster. Everyone who joins a team completes 24 hours of basic training first, then settles into a unit. This protects the people in the field — and you.

Process

Four steps from application to the field

  1. Step 01

    Apply

    Fill in the form below. We reply within ten working days and work out the right unit together.

  2. Step 02

    Intro conversation

    Online, around 30 minutes. We talk through expectations and the time you can give.

  3. Step 03

    Basic training

    24 hours over three weekends: disaster basics, code of ethics, child protection, psychological first aid.

  4. Step 04

    Join a unit

    You are paired with an experienced volunteer and work alongside them for the first three months.

Units

Which team you could join

You don't need a background in health or psychology. What most of our teams need most is steady time and care with detail.

What we ask for

  • Being 18 or over
  • At least 8 hours a month, regularly
  • Accepting the code of ethics and child protection policy
  • A criminal record declaration for units working with children

Field team

Needs assessment, registration, running child-friendly spaces. Intense during a response, drills and preparedness the rest of the time.

Training team

Trainer or assistant in school and neighbourhood sessions. Requires weekday daytime availability.

Specialist pool

Psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, child development specialists. On call during a response.

Remote support

Translation, data entry, design, software, communications. You can contribute from anywhere.

Logistics and warehouse

Stock counts, shipment planning, vehicle support. At our Nicosia and Famagusta warehouses.

Membership

Volunteering and membership are not the same thing

A volunteer works in the field. A member votes at the general assembly, stands for the boards and sets the association's direction. You can do both.

Membership requirements

  • Being 18 or over with legal capacity
  • Not being subject to any of the barriers set out in the statute
  • A written recommendation from two existing members
  • A board decision within 30 days
  • Annual fee: ₺1,200 (students and pensioners: ₺400)
