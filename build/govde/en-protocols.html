Protocols

Steps to follow in the field for psychologists, counsellors, social workers, health workers, teachers and trained volunteers. Every protocol is based on international guidelines; its sources are listed on its own page.

Field protocols

Protocols prepared for use in the field

  • Psychological first aid A step-by-step framework for non-specialist volunteers. Handbook
  • Child-friendly space Set-up, safeguarding and daily routine. Protocol
  • Psychosocial needs assessment form A structured assessment for field teams. Form
  • School preparedness curriculum Three lesson plans for teachers. Curriculum
  • Secondary trauma for field workers Signs, post-shift decompression, team support. Protocol
  • Incident page template For publishing verified information and support points when an event occurs. Template

Protocols are applied together with the association’s ethical principles: do no harm, consent, confidentiality and working within one’s competence. To apply them in the field, join our training.
