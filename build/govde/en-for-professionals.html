6.2

Ethical principles

  • Do no harm: every intervention is assessed in advance for possible harm.
  • Informed consent and voluntariness.
  • Confidentiality and its limits — explained openly.
  • Working within one’s competence; referring when needed.
  • Non-discrimination, political neutrality.
  • Never using the images or stories of those supported without consent.

Full ethics and confidentiality principles

6.3

Caring for responders and secondary traumatisation

Those who witness others’ suffering are affected too. This is not a professional failure but the nature of the work; the response is organisational, not individual: rotation, supervision, peer support.

Protocol Secondary trauma in field workers Signs, after-shift decompression, team support.
