03

Statement of autonomy

  • No funding body or donor has any say over the content of the association’s publications, training or psychosocial support work.
  • The association is independent of political parties, candidates and campaigns; its name may not be used for commercial or personal promotion.
  • Partnerships are set up on terms that protect the association’s ethical principles and scientific independence.
  • Decisions are taken by the bodies defined in the statute; members set the association’s direction at the General Assembly.

This statement is published with the approval of the association’s Representative Body. Governance: Governance and statute.
