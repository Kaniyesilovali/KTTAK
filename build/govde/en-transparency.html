We give the account before it is asked for

An association earns trust by publishing documents, not by declaring good intentions. What we publish and when is set out below — as a commitment, not a preference.

Our commitment

Minutes and election results
Kept current
Board of directors and audit board members
Kept current

Nothing we publish is later removed — including the difficult years. When a document is superseded, the previous version stays in the archive.

Independence

Whose money we take, and whose we don't

Not depending on a single source is a condition of our independence. No donor has a say over programme content — a rule written into our gift acceptance policy, with no exceptions.

We accept

  • Individual donations and membership fees
  • Unconditional corporate and foundation grants
  • International funding programmes
  • Training and consultancy income

We refuse

  • Arms, tobacco, gambling and fossil fuel sectors
  • Donations conditional on influencing programme content
  • Large anonymous gifts or funds of unexplained origin
  • Party-linked donations during election periods

Independent audit

Our financial statements are audited by a sworn financial advisor. The audit opinion is annexed to the annual report in full, unabridged.

Public oversight

As required by the Associations Law (23/2016), our annual declaration and general assembly results are filed with the Nicosia District Office and can be verified against our registry record.

Reporting line

Report suspected misconduct, conflicts of interest or ethical breaches to etik@kttak.org. Reports go directly to the audit board.
