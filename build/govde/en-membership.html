Become a member and help shape our direction

The association grows stronger with its members. Members vote at the General Assembly, stand for the association's bodies and take part in the working groups.

Who can join

Membership requirements

Membership requirements are set out in article 6 of the statute. The full list will appear here once the registered statute is published.

Volunteering and membership are not the same

A member takes part in the association's decisions. A volunteer takes on roles in our work after completing the training. You can do both. Volunteering

Process

How applications are decided

  1. Step 01

    Application

    You fill in the membership application form.

  2. Step 02

    Decision

    Your application is decided within 30 days at the latest (statute art. 7).

  3. Step 03

    Notification

    You are notified of the decision in writing.
