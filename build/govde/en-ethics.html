Ethics and confidentiality

The safety, dignity and privacy of the person receiving support come before everything. These principles apply to all of the association’s work, publications and this website.

Ethical principles

The principles that guide our work

Based on the working principles in Article 5 of the statute and on international humanitarian standards.

Do no harm

All work is assessed for its potential to cause harm as much as its potential to help. Good intentions are not enough.

Consent and voluntariness

No one is pressed to talk, to meet or to take part in research. Consent is sought in plain language and can be withdrawn.

Privacy

The faces, voices, names and stories of those supported are never used without consent. Photos of children, and images of destruction, injury or suffering from disaster areas, are not used.

Confidentiality and its limits

What is shared is kept confidential. The only exception is when the person’s or someone else’s life is in danger; this limit is explained clearly at the start.

Competence

Everyone works within their training and experience. Situations requiring specialist care are referred to the appropriate service.

Neutrality and non-discrimination

Support is given regardless of language, religion, ethnicity, gender, political opinion or any other distinction. No political party, candidate or campaign content appears.

Verified information

Unverified information about an event is never shared as fact. When an error is noticed it is corrected openly.

Those in need come first

The association’s visibility never takes precedence over the needs of those it supports.

Privacy

Your personal data

Personal data collected on this site and through our forms is processed under the Personal Data Protection Law (89/2007). Details: Privacy and personal data · Cookie policy.
