5.2

During and right after an event

  • During an earthquake: Drop, cover, hold on. Do not run outside; keep away from windows and heavy furniture.
  • On a fire or flood warning: follow official evacuation instructions without delay; do not cross flooded roads.
  • Right after: check for injuries, call 112, let your loved ones know. Do not share unverified information.
  • In the first days: keep up sleep and meals, keep children close, limit the news.

Who it is for

Parents, teachers, workplaces and young people.

Parents

Children draw strength from adults’ calm. Short, honest, age-appropriate explanations; routine and plenty of closeness.

Talking to your child

Teachers

After a crisis, school is the first normal setting for children. Opening a safe space to talk in class and noticing the pupil who needs support.

School preparedness curriculum

Workplaces

When staff return to work after an event, productivity may drop; flexibility, open communication and a named contact person speed recovery.

Psychological first aid

Young people

Friends and social media can be both support and burden. Sharing feelings, stepping away from the screen when needed, and telling false information apart.

5.4

Frequently asked questions

Are my feelings normal?+

After an abnormal event, sleep problems, startling, restlessness and absent-mindedness are very common and ease for most people within a few weeks. Which reactions are normal?

When should I see a professional?+

If reactions last longer than four weeks, if you cannot manage daily tasks, or if you have thoughts of harming yourself. In the last case call 112 without waiting.

Is TAK-DER a crisis line?+

No. TAK-DER is a professional association; in an emergency call 112, for violence call 155. Where to turn

Should I tell my child what happened?+

Yes — briefly, honestly and in a way that suits their age. Children often fill silence with scenarios that are scarier than the truth. Example phrases

Can I use these materials in my organisation?+

Yes. All TAK-DER publications may be copied and adapted for non-commercial purposes with attribution (CC BY-NC 4.0).

How can I trust what I see on social media?+

Before sharing, check the source, the date and statements from official institutions. Four-step verification
