Governance

The association's bodies

Statute art. 13

General Assembly

The association's highest decision-making body; made up of the members, it elects the other bodies.

Statute arts. 20–21

Representative Body

10 full and 5 substitute members. Roles: Coordinator, Deputy Coordinator, Secretary and Treasurer.

Statute arts. 20, 25

Audit Body

3 full and 1 substitute member; oversees the association's work and accounts.
