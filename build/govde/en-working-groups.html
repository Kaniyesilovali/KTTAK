Working groups

The association's work is carried out in the working and advisory groups provided for in article 26 of the statute. Members can join the groups that match their interests and expertise; every skill makes the network stronger.

Research and Training

Planning research and preparing training programmes and materials.

Ethics and Scientific Advisory

Overseeing the scientific basis of our work and its compliance with ethical principles.

Psychosocial Support and Field

Planning and delivering support work and field activities.

Community Work

Awareness, preparedness and resilience work with communities.

Communication and Documentation

The association's announcements, publications and archive, and applying our ethical communication principles.

Projects

Developing and writing project proposals and following their delivery.

Fundraising

Developing resources so the association can sustain its work.

External Relations

Relations with institutions, professional bodies and national and international networks.

Active groups and their coordinators will be announced on this page as they are established.
