If you are in an emergency

If life is in danger, or you are worried about your own or someone else’s safety, do not wait: call one of the numbers below now.

Emergency numbers in North Cyprus

  • 112 Emergency / Ambulance
  • 155 Police
  • 101 Civil Defence
  • 177 Forest fire

Hospital: you can go directly to the emergency department of the nearest hospital.

TAK-DER is not a crisis line or emergency service

We do not provide emergency response, and we cannot answer messages from this site, email or social media immediately. If life is in danger, call the emergency numbers directly.
