Accessibility statement

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including in a crisis.

Target

WCAG 2.1 AA

  • Text and background colours meet at least 4.5:1 contrast.
  • All pages can be used with a keyboard; focus is always visible.
  • Forms use visible labels.
  • Animations switch off for users who prefer reduced motion.
  • Pages are built with headings and landmarks for screen readers.
  • Guides are designed to be printable.

Known limitations

Not yet complete

  • Accessibility checks of PDF documents to be uploaded have not yet been done.
  • The site is currently in Turkish and English only.

Feedback

If you meet a barrier

If you cannot access part of the site, write to {{EPOSTA}} and we will try to provide the content in a form that suits you.
