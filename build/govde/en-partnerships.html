National and international partnerships

No institution is enough on its own in disasters and crises. We work with public bodies, local authorities, universities, schools, professional bodies and civil society, and with national and international networks.

National

Institutions we work with on the island

Joint training, preparedness and research with public bodies, local authorities, universities, schools, professional bodies and civil society organisations (statute art. 4/7).

International

Networks and a common language

Taking part in national and international networks (statute art. 4/9). Our work uses UNDRR’s Sendai Framework and IASC and WHO guidelines as a common language.

Signed partnerships will be listed here with the institutions’ consent.

For institutions

What we can do together

Training

Psychosocial support, psychological first aid or secondary-trauma training for your staff.

Joint projects and research

Needs assessments, effectiveness evaluations and joint publications.

Preparedness plans

Developing a psychosocial preparedness plan together for a school, municipality or workplace.
