Who we are

A professional association that stands alongside people in hard times

Travma Afet Kriz Derneği (TAK-DER) — the Trauma, Disaster and Crisis Association — is a science-based, ethical professional association working on psychosocial support in trauma, disaster and crisis. We are based in Nicosia (Lefkoşa).

We aim to strengthen individuals, families and communities; to support the psychosocial work of public institutions and civil society organisations; and to build professional capacity through research and scientific knowledge.

Are you in an emergency right now?

TAK-DER is not a crisis line or an emergency response service. If a life is in danger, or you are worried about your own or someone else's safety, please call the emergency lines now or go to the emergency department of the nearest hospital.
