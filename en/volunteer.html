Volunteer — training first, then a role

Volunteering in trauma, disaster and crisis takes more than good intentions. TAK-DER volunteers complete the association's training before taking on any role. That protects both the people receiving support and you.

Process

From application to role

  1. Step 01

    Application

    You fill in the form below.

  2. Step 02

    Training

    You complete the association's mandatory training. The programme will be announced on the training page.

  3. Step 03

    Role

    You take on a role in a working group or at an event.

Requirements

Volunteering requirements

Volunteering is governed by article 12 of the statute. The full list of requirements will appear here once the registered statute is published. The principles alongside apply to every volunteer from today.

What we ask of every volunteer

  • Complete the association's training before taking on a role
  • Follow our working principles
  • Protect the privacy of the people we support; never share photos, voices, names or stories without consent
  • Never make public statements on the association's behalf or share unverified information on social media

Apply

Volunteer application form

Filling in the form commits you to nothing. Your application goes to the association's official email address and is used only for the volunteering process.

Questions: info@tak-der.org

If you want a say in the association's decisions, see membership.
