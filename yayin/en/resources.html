Content note. Some of the guides below describe disaster experiences, injury and loss. Stopping partway is entirely reasonable. TAKDER is not a crisis line; in an emergency call 112 or see where to turn.

Sources

Where the information on this page comes from

The methods and standards below are adapted from international humanitarian and mental health literature; anything specific to the TRNC comes from official government sources. The association's own programme decisions — training hours, follow-up periods, team ratios, fee policy — are not listed here: those are choices, not citations.

Family and individual guides
World Health Organization, War Trauma Foundation and World Vision International (2011). Psychological First Aid: Guide for Field Workers. Geneva: WHO. who.int
National Child Traumatic Stress Network and National Center for PTSD (2006). Psychological First Aid: Field Operations Guide, 2nd edition. nctsn.org
TRNC Civil Defence Organisation. Beklenilmeyene Hazır Olmak — household preparedness booklet (Turkish). PDF
Earthquake Country Alliance. Seven Steps to Earthquake Safety — non-structural risk reduction. earthquakecountry.org
Organisational protocols
World Health Organization, War Trauma Foundation and World Vision International (2011). Psychological First Aid: Guide for Field Workers. Geneva: WHO. who.int
Inter-Agency Standing Committee (2007). IASC Guidelines on Mental Health and Psychosocial Support in Emergency Settings. Geneva: IASC. interagencystandingcommittee.org
Sphere Association (2018). The Sphere Handbook: Humanitarian Charter and Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Response, 4th edition. Geneva. spherestandards.org
The Alliance for Child Protection in Humanitarian Action (2019). Minimum Standards for Child Protection in Humanitarian Action (CPMS), 2019 edition. alliancecpha.org
Inter-agency Network for Education in Emergencies (2024). INEE Minimum Standards for Education: Preparedness, Response, Recovery, 2024 edition. inee.org

Links go to the publisher of each source; last checked 10 September 2026. If you spot an error in a citation, tell us and we will fix it.
