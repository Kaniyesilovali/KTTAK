Who we are

We are a science-based, ethical professional association working on psychosocial support in trauma, disaster and crisis.

We aim to strengthen individuals, families and communities; to support the psychosocial work of public institutions and civil society organisations; and to build professional capacity through research. We are based in Nicosia (Lefkoşa).

Our aims and values

Principles that guide us

From article 5 of our statute

  • Current scientific evidence
  • Privacy
  • Do no harm
  • Non-discrimination
  • Human rights and peace
  • Political neutrality
  • People in need come first

What we do

From preparedness to recovery, alongside you at every stage

Our working model

Preparedness and prevention

Risk and needs assessment, preparedness and protective work.

Psychosocial support

Planning and delivering support work, including in disaster areas when needed.

Training and capacity building

Training for the public and for professionals.

Support for workers

Training, supervision and self-care for field workers and volunteers.

Research and publications

Research, reports, practice guides and training material.

Ethical communication and awareness

Accurate information, privacy and ethical use of media.

Are you in an emergency right now?

TAK-DER is not a crisis line or an emergency response service. If a life is in danger, or you are worried about your own or someone else's safety, call the emergency lines now or go to the emergency department of the nearest hospital.
