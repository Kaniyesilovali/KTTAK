Training and workshops

Knowledge grows when it is shared. We are preparing training programmes for professionals working in disaster and crisis, and for the public. Programmes and dates will be announced here as they are confirmed.

For professionals

Certificate programmes

Science-based, ethical certificate programmes on psychosocial support in trauma, disaster and crisis.

For disaster and crisis workers

Workshops

Practical workshops for disaster and crisis workers from psychology, psychological counselling and guidance, and social work; supervision and self-care.

For the public

Awareness training

Sessions for schools, institutions and communities on preparedness, psychological first aid and accurate information.

Calendar

Training calendar

The training programme is in preparation.

Programmes, dates and application details will be announced here and on our social media accounts.
